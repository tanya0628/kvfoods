Our Company

Legacy

❧

KRISHNA VASUDEVA BHARAT FOODS PRIVATE LIMITED feels proud to carry forward the legacy of the legendary firm Bal Kishan Om Prakash, established in 1936 at Sri Ganganagar, Rajasthan.

Our journey is built on trust, quality and experience in agriculture and commodity trading. We continue to honour our heritage while serving today's families.
